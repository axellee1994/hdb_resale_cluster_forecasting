What this project does

Clusters 125 HDB (town, flat-type) combinations into three segments — Mature & Affordable, Newer High-Growth, and Premium — using K-Means on median price, growth rate, and remaining lease.

Tests whether adding the cluster assignment as a feature improves a HistGradientBoosting price model. Result: a modest but consistent improvement (MAE drops ~3.3%), confirming the clusters capture signal beyond what raw town and flat-type encodings provide.
