HDB Resale Flat Clustering & Price Forecasting

Unsupervised segmentation of Singapore HDB towns by price behavior, with an honest test of whether clustering improves price prediction.

Interactive Dashboard

Tableau Public dashboard will be embedded here after publishing.

What this project does

Clusters HDB towns and flat-types by price behavior — median price, growth rate, remaining lease — so a buyer can find towns that behave similarly to the one they are looking at, but at a different price point.

The real contribution: testing whether adding the cluster assignment as a feature actually improves a price forecasting model, trained and backtested the same way with and without it. Either result is a legitimate finding.
